# YouTube Immersive privacy policy

YouTube Immersive is an independent Chrome extension by aste2d. It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

The extension runs on `www.youtube.com`. It reads the current page path and interacts with the video player and recommendations on the page so it can apply your layout and shortcut choices. It does not record which pages or videos you visit.

The extension stores your shortcut and feature preferences in Chrome's local extension storage and the player-width preference in YouTube's local site storage. It keeps these settings on your device until you reset them or clear the corresponding storage. Uninstalling the extension removes its Chrome extension storage. Clearing site data for `www.youtube.com` clears the player-width preference along with other YouTube site data.

The extension does not send your settings or activity to the developer or other parties. It has no analytics, tracking, or developer-operated service. YouTube operates under its own privacy policy.

For privacy questions, use the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/aste2d/youtube-immersive/issues).
